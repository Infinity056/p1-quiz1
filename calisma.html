Relative (adjective) clauses: who / which / that

Hafta 8 · CC Booklet "Adjective Clauses 1" · Revision Ex. 1–2

Bir ismi tanımlayan, ismin hemen arkasından gelen cümlecik.

  • who → insanlar. The woman who lives next door is a nurse.
  • which → eşyalar, hayvanlar. The book which is on the table is mine.
  • that → ikisi için de olur. The man that helped us… / The phone that I bought…
DurumNasıl anlarım?Zamir atılır mı?
Subjectwho/which/that'ten hemen sonra fiil gelir.
The girl who sits next to me…		Atılamaz
Objectwho/which/that'ten sonra yeni özne + fiil gelir.
The film (that) we watched…		Atılabilir (Ø)

Cümle birleştirme (Revision Ex. 2 tipi): ikinci cümledeki tekrar eden kelimeyi (he, she, it, him, them) sil, yerine zamiri koy ve ismin hemen arkasına yerleştir.

The man works at our school. You met him at the party. → The man (who/that/Ø) you met at the party works at our school.

The woman is a scientist. She discovered a new medicine. → The woman who discovered a new medicine is a scientist.

Tuzaklar
  • Fazladan zamir bırakma: The shoes I bought them were expensive. → The shoes I bought were expensive.
  • İnsan için which: The man which helped us → who / that
  • Fiil, nitelenen isme uyar: I have a neighbor who doesn't like music. / two sons who love football. who sit → The girl who sits
  • Ana cümlenin fiili ilk isme uyar: The students who passed the exam were happy.
  • Subject konumundaki zamiri silme: The woman lives next door is a singer. → The woman who lives next door is a singer.

Too / very / enough

Hafta 7 · CC Booklet "Too/Enough/Very" · Revision Ex. 6
  • very + sıfat: sadece vurgu, yapılabiliyor. The hotel was very expensive, but we managed to afford it.
  • too + sıfat (+ to V1): gereğinden fazla, yapılamıyor. The coffee is too hot. I can't drink it.
  • sıfat + enough: yeterince. He isn't old enough to drive.
  • enough + isim: We don't have enough chairs.
Karar kuralı

İkinci cümleye bak. "Yapamıyorum, kimse cevaplayamadı, kısar mısın" gibi bir olumsuz sonuç varsa → too. "But we managed, I really enjoyed it" gibi olumlu sonuç varsa → very.

Subject–verb agreement (özne–fiil uyumu)

CC Booklet: Subject–Verb Agreement 1 (8 kural)
  1. Tek kişi/şey → tekil fiil: My mother drives to work.
  2. İki veya daha fazla → çoğul fiil: My parents drive to work.
  3. Düzensiz çoğullar (children, people, women, men) → çoğul fiil: People are worried.
  4. and ile bağlanan iki özne → çoğul fiil: Tom and Jack play basketball. My sister and I walk to school.
  5. Öznenin arkasında iki fiil varsa ikisi de uyar: My brother plays football and watches TV.
  6. Gruptan bir kişi → tekil fiil: One of my friends has a new bike.
  7. in / of / at … ile uzayan özne → ana isme bak: People from my country are friendly. The plates in the kitchen are dirty. The new teacher at our school helps students.
  8. every-, some-, any-, no- zamirleri → tekil fiil: Everybody seems happy. Does anyone have a pen?

Soru kurma

CC Booklet: Question Formation · FoG2 Units 2, 9, 10, 16, 20 · High Note 1, 5
  • Kalıp: (Wh-) + yardımcı fiil + özne + fiil. How often do you speak English? What are you feeling right now?
  • be ile: be başa gelir. Where is he from? Were you at home last night?
  • Altı çizili bilgiye soru (booklet Ex. 4): He trains two hours a day. → How long does he train a day? · He swims 6 km a week. → How many kilometers does he swim a week? · He is 170 cm tall. → How tall is he?
  • Wh- kelimeleri: what (ne), where (nerede), when (ne zaman), who (kim), whose (kimin), why (neden), how (nasıl), how often (ne sıklıkla), how much / how many (ne kadar / kaç), what kind of (ne tür).
  • Kısa cevaplar: Yes, I do. / No, she doesn't. / Yes, they are. / No, I wasn't. / Yes, I can.
Tuzaklar
  • Soruda fiil yalın kalır: Does he likes → Does he like, Did you went → Did you go.
  • Özneyi soruyorsan yardımcı fiil yok: Who lives next door? (özne) / Who did you call? (nesne)

Bölüm 3

Sınavdan önce son 5 dakika

  1. Relative clause'da who/which/that'ten sonra it, them, him, her kalmasın. İnsan için which yok.
  2. Comparative'de than, superlative'de the. Asla "more + -er".
  3. Olumsuz sonuç varsa too, sadece vurguysa very.
  4. Everyone / nobody / one of the… → tekil fiil (is, was, needs).
  5. think, have, look: fikir, sahiplik, görünüş → -ing yok.
  6. Kelime boşluğunda önce türü bul: the + isim, very + sıfat, to + fiil.
  7. Booklet türetme tabloları: leadership, advice/advise, inspiration, organizational, succeed, profit, influence, encouraging, death, dedication · builder, decorative, survive/survivor, strength, robbery, famous, origin.
  8. Form değişmeli mi? Özne he/she → -s, geçmiş → -ed, is/are → -ing. upset'in geçmişi yine upset.
  9. found → founded; wake → woke; leave → left; fall → fell.
  10. equipment, information, advice çoğul olmaz.
